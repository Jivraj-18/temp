# Demo Script — Institutional Profiles (Contact Scraping)

**Demo page:** [`institutional-profiles.html`](institutional-profiles.html) · Live: <https://jivraj-18.github.io/temp/demos/institutional-profiles.html>
**Run time:** ~4 minutes · **Three phases, five steps**

> **How to read this script:** each phase has a **Do** line (what to click on screen) and a **Say** line (what to tell the audience). Read the *Say* text as-is or paraphrase it.

---

## Phase 1 — Executive overview & ruleset (Steps 1–2)

**Do:** Switch to the Institutional Profiles dashboard. Point to the top-level **Run at a Glance** KPI cards.

**Say:**
> "Now let's move to our Institutional Profiles engine, where we transform unstructured web data into verified corporate profiles. Right at the top, the Run at a Glance banner gives you immediate visibility into total scale, successful outcomes, and machine processing time. Before a single webpage is scraped, the engine locks onto a Confirmed Brief — visible here. This defines the exact title requirements and field parameters governing the run, ensuring the AI only extracts highly relevant target leads."

## Phase 2 — Entity resolution & delivery (Steps 3–4)

**Do:** Scroll to the **Company Resolution** table. Click the **Contact Delivery** tab and use a dropdown filter.

**Say:**
> "The hardest part of web scraping is ensuring a website actually belongs to the right company. Our Company Resolution module tracked 19 target entities, automatically handling naming variations and domain redirects to successfully map contacts. Here in the Contact Delivery panel, we see the final output: 54 highly targeted contacts. I can use these dropdown filters to instantly sort by company name or specific C-suite positions, proving how quickly unstructured web text turns into clean, actionable CRM data."

## Phase 3 — Auditing & quality control (Step 5)

**Do:** Click **View Evidence** next to any contact to reveal the source snippet and the **Rejects / Held** log.

**Say:**
> "What sets this platform apart is that we never provide data without proof. If I click 'View Evidence' on this contact, the platform shows the exact source URL and the specific text snippet where the machine found the info. Next to it, our Exceptions Log shows you every record that was rejected or held back. If a profile didn't meet our strict confidence thresholds, it wasn't hidden — it was quarantined here for audit. This guarantees a 100% traceable, audit-ready data stream that your operations team can completely trust."

---

## Key numbers to remember

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Targets covered | 19 target companies |
| Validated output | 54 executive contacts |
| Run governed by | A rule-based, human-confirmed brief |
| Traceability | Every field links back to its source text snippet |
