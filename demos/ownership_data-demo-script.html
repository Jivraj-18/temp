Demo Script: Institutional Holdings & Beneficial Ownership Profiles

STEP 1 OF 9: One profile, two kinds of disclosure

  • Presenter Action: Point to the two primary data sections displayed side-by-side on the profile layout.
  • Script: "Welcome to the core profile view. Here, we present institutional 13F holdings and Schedule 13D/G beneficial ownership side by side for a single issuer. Crucially, these data streams are never added together. Because each dataset originates from completely different filing types with distinct compliance rules, keeping them autonomous prevents double-counting and maintains data integrity."

STEP 2 OF 9: Pick an issuer

  • Presenter Action: Click the issuer dropdown menu and select Genco Shipping. Point to the resulting data rows.
  • Script: "Selecting a specific issuer immediately aligns the entire profile. Let's look at Genco Shipping. Notice how every table below instantly filters to this selection, pulling in eight distinct 13D/G observations across the original filing and all subsequent amendments."

STEP 3 OF 9: Replay how the profile was built

  • Presenter Action: Hover over the timeline control or click the 'Replay' feature, scrubbing from December 2023 to June 2026.
  • Script: "To understand our data's evolution, we use the Replay feature. This reconstructs the profile chronologically based on when the SEC officially accepted each filing—spanning from December 2023 to June 2026. This ensures you only ever see what was factually known at that exact point in time."

STEP 4 OF 9: What is asserted, and what is not

  • Presenter Action: Point out a verified "Safe row" and contrast it against a flagged diagnostic row with empty value fields.
  • Script: "Transparency is vital. Verified 'Safe rows' are fully backed by source provenance and confidently asserted. Conversely, any record that fails our strict automated checks remains visible as a diagnostic tool for review, ensuring no unverified values are accidentally claimed."

STEP 5 OF 9: 13F institutional holdings

  • Presenter Action: Scroll to the 13F Institutional Holdings table and highlight an amended chain of rows.
  • Script: "Moving to institutional positions, the platform tracks reported positions through resolved 13F chains—intelligently capturing amendments and corrections. A position is only officially asserted once its complete historical chain is fully resolved by the engine."

STEP 6 OF 9: Evidence for every value

  • Presenter Action: Click to expand an individual evidence row, revealing the metadata panel and the SEC hyperlink.
  • Script: "Every single value on this page is fully auditable. By opening an evidence row, you can view the raw text, the normalized value, source file, selector coordinates, byte ranges, and its unique SHA-256 hash. We also provide a direct link straight to the official SEC filing for manual verification."

STEP 7 OF 9: When each fact became known

  • Presenter Action: Point to the timestamp column labelled 'SEC Acceptance Time'.
  • Script: "We don't overwrite history. By tracking the exact SEC acceptance time, we log precisely when a fact became market knowledge. Amendments and corrections are preserved as independent events, giving you a flawless audit trail of how information unfolded."

STEP 8 OF 9: Kept apart on purpose

  • Presenter Action: Point to the group observations section and highlight a flagged record under review.
  • Script: "To avoid misleading aggregations, group observations are never blended into an individual's total ownership. If a record requires an analyst's eye, it explicitly lists the reasons and required next steps, keeping unverified figures safe from exposure."

STEP 9 OF 9: The boundary of the claim

  • Presenter Action: Scroll to the bottom disclaimer or informational banner regarding scope boundaries.
  • Script: "Finally, we believe in defining our guardrails. This view is a bounded pilot, not a global, SEC-wide dataset or a declaration of absolute ownership. The platform clearly states these boundaries out right, leaving no room for assumptions."
