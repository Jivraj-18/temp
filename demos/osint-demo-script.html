How DeepSeek OSINT Works

  1. Intelligent Data Ingestion
    • Procedure: Our Agentic AI continuously monitors and ingests vast volumes of unstructured data (e.g., public records, leaked documents, web dumps, dark web forums) from diverse open-source intelligence (OSINT) channels.
    • Benefit: Ensures comprehensive, real-time coverage beyond traditional data feeds,
  2. LLM-Powered Entity Extraction & Contextual Analysis
    • Procedure: Leveraging state-of-the-art LLMs, the system instantly processes this raw data to identify and extract critical entities (names, organizations, locations, IP addresses, dates, relationships) and contextualize complex narratives, far beyond keyword searches.
    • Benefit: Streamlines investigation, interprets nuances, and uncovers connections traditional methods miss,
  3. Actionable Risk Unmasking & Synthesis
    • Procedure: The Agentic AI then cross-references, correlates, and synthesizes these findings, revealing obscured corporate actions, hidden affiliations, and potential sanction violations. It presents these as clear, prioritized, and actionable intelligence,
    • Benefit: Provides precise, risk-mitigating insights directly relevant to MSCI's governance and index integrity teams,

The "Input Page" – Tells What to Find

Step 1 - Enter Entity Details

  • Procedure - Provide the subject's core identifier (e.g., name, registration number, unique ID) for a targeted investigation.

Step 2 - Select Subject Type

  • Procedure - Choose the precise classification (e.g., Individual, Financial Institution, Vessel) to optimize search algorithms and data sources.

Step 3 - Specify Location (Optional)

  • Procedure - Enter the known geographical location to narrow the search. Leave blank for a broader, global analysis.

Step 4 - Initiate OSINT Scan

  • Procedure - Trigger the deep-seek OSINT scan. The engine will leverage integrated APIs (e.g., Open Sanctions, ICIJ Offshore Leaks, Open Corporates) for comprehensive data aggregation and analysis,

Result Page -Synthesized Intelligence for Decision Making

This page provides a structured, actionable OSINT synthesis designed to facilitate rapid, high-confidence risk assessment.

1. Subject Identity & Risk Profile

  • Purpose - Establishes the subject’s classification and immediate risk posture.
  • Procedure
    • Confirm Identity: Validate subject type (Individual/Entity/vessel/bank/NGO etc.) against primary identifiers.
    • Assign Risk Level: Apply a color-coded severity indicator (Low/Medium/High/Critical) based on aggregated data points.

2. Operational Domicile Mapping

  • Purpose - Visualizes the subject’s global footprint and regulatory exposure.
  • Procedure:
    • Identify Jurisdictions: Extract all registered, operational, and suspected locations.
    • Contextualize: Map these locations against known regulatory environments to highlight compliance complexity.

3. Intelligence Synthesis & Typology Analysis

  • Purpose - Provides a narrative history of conduct and a quantitative breakdown of risk categories.
  • Procedure:
    • Summarize Findings: Synthesize criminal history, litigation, and settlements into a concise narrative.
    • Weight Typology: Calculate the prevalence of specific offense types (e.g., financial crime, regulatory breach) to quantify risk focus.

4. Reputational Risk Assessment

  • Purpose - Evaluates public perception and potential brand damage via negative media.
  • Procedure:
    • Aggregate Media: Consolidate reports from news outlets, blogs, and public forums.
    • Sentiment Analysis: Filter for derogatory content to determine the severity of reputational impact.

5. Regulatory & Jurisdictional Guidance

  • Purpose - Outlines official stances and compliance requirements from local authorities.
  • Procedure:
    • Review Advisories: Extract official warnings, sanctions, or recommendations issued by local regulatory bodies.
    • Action Mapping: Translate these findings into actionable compliance or mitigation steps.

6. Source Transparency & Verification

  • Purpose - Ensures auditability and provides a foundation for independent validation.
  • Procedure:
    • Log Sources: List all primary data repositories, OSINT APIs, and public records accessed.
    • Verify Integrity: Provide direct links or citations for each data point to support internal verification processes.

Way Forward

Achieving optimal performance necessitates further training of the engine on complex search parameters. This training will specifically address the intricate task of managing name permutations, aliases, and AKAs, integrating advanced fuzzy logic. The matching process will adopt a structured, multi-tiered approach: commencing with primary exact match identification, progressing to a secondary exact match, and concluding with the refinement of category assumptions to minimize both false positives and false negatives.
